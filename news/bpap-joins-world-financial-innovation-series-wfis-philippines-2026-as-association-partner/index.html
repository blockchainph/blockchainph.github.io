BPAP Joins World Financial Innovation Series (WFIS) Philippines 2026 as Association Partner

July 29, 2026
By BPAP Secretariat

The Blockchain Practitioners Association of the Philippines (BPAP) has joined the World Financial Innovation Series (WFIS) Philippines 2026 as an Association Partner, extending its advocacy for sound blockchain policy and practice into the country's mainstream financial services landscape.

Now in its fifth edition, WFIS Philippines brings together senior leaders from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), banks, fintech companies, regulators, and technology providers to discuss the future of financial services, digital transformation, cybersecurity, compliance, and emerging technologies. The summit will be held on 25-26 August 2026 at the Manila Marriott Hotel.

As an Association Partner, BPAP joins other industry organizations in supporting meaningful dialogue on financial innovation, where blockchain technology, digital assets, and evolving regulatory frameworks continue to play an increasingly important role in shaping the future of the Philippine financial sector.

BPAP looks forward to collaborating with financial institutions, regulators, technology partners, and fellow industry stakeholders through this partnership. The association will continue to support initiatives that promote blockchain education, responsible innovation, and industry collaboration in the Philippines.

BPAP looks forward to a productive partnership with the World Financial Innovation Series and Tradepass in advancing innovation and strengthening the country's digital financial ecosystem.
